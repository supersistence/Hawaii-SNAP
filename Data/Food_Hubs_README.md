# Food Hubs SNAP & Double Bucks Data (2021-2024)

## Overview

This dataset tracks SNAP benefits and Double Bucks incentive program sales through Hawaii's food hub network from 2021 to 2024.

## Data Source

**Citation:** Fardkhales, Lincoln, and Heaivilin (forthcoming)

Food hub sales data includes:
- SNAP EBT transactions at participating food hubs
- Double Bucks incentive matching funds
- Combined across Hawaii's food hub network

## Files

- **Food_Hubs_SNAP_Double_Bucks_2021-2024.csv** - Raw data in CSV format
- **../visualizations/food_hubs_visualization.html** - Standalone interactive visualization

## Data Dictionary

| Column | Type | Description |
|--------|------|-------------|
| Year | Integer | Calendar year (2021-2024) |
| SNAP Benefits ($) | Integer | Total SNAP EBT transactions at food hubs |
| Double Bucks ($) | Integer | Total Double Bucks incentive matching funds |
| Total Impact ($) | Integer | Combined SNAP + Double Bucks sales |

## Key Findings

### Growth Metrics

- **8.2x increase** in SNAP benefits: $136K (2021) → $1.1M (2024)
- **4.4x increase** in Double Bucks: $109K (2021) → $479K (2024)
- **6.5x increase** in total impact: $245K (2021) → $1.6M (2024)

### 2024 Snapshot

- **SNAP Benefits:** $1,117,000
- **Double Bucks:** $479,000 (43% of SNAP sales)
- **Total Impact:** $1,596,000

### Policy Implications

1. **Rapid Growth:** Demonstrates strong demand for local food among SNAP recipients
2. **Incentive Multiplier:** Double Bucks effectively increases purchasing power by 43%
3. **Scaling Opportunity:** Food hub sales still represent <0.3% of Hawaii's $720M+ annual SNAP spending
4. **Local Agriculture:** Supports Hawaii farmers while providing fresh food access

## Double Bucks Program

The Double Bucks program is an incentive initiative that matches SNAP purchases at participating food hubs, effectively doubling SNAP recipients' purchasing power for locally-grown produce and products.

**Example:**
- Customer spends $20 in SNAP benefits
- Receives $20 in Double Bucks to spend on local produce
- Total purchasing power: $40
